  Row 20: Remarks: 
 VIII. Attachments (if applicable) | (For future activity improvements.) 
 1. Program Flow and Time Management 
 Observations: 
 The program generally followed the planned schedule; however, minor delays occurred at the start of the event due to heavy rain. As a result, both the participants and the speaker arrived later than expected, which caused the initial sessions to be pushed back slightly. 
 Recommendations: 
 Consider adjusting call time or having a flexible buffer period in future events, especially during seasons with unpredictable weather. 
 Provide early announcements or reminders to participants regarding possible delays due to weather conditions so they can adjust travel time accordingly. 
 2. Participant Engagement and Interaction 
 Observations: 
 Students showed high interest, especially during discussions on digital forensics, cybersecurity careers, and ethical decisionmaking. 
 Handson or scenariobased portions received the strongest engagement. 
 Recommendations: 
 Consider integrating more interactive components such as short demos, simulations, or quick-response activities. 
 Include structured Q&A portions after each section to maximize learning while maintaining order. 
 Use digital tools (QR polls, live questions, etc.) to encourage real-time interaction. 
 3. Technical Setup and Venue Preparation 
 Observations: 
 Audiovisual equipment was generally functional, though minor adjustments to sound clarity were noted. 
 Seating arrangements accommodated participants well, but visibility from the back rows was occasionally a challenge. 
 Recommendations: 
 Conduct a full technical run-through at least an hour before the program to validate visuals, audio, and connectivity and used a well function speaker and microphone. 
 Assign a technical support team to stand by throughout the event. 
 Consider improving screen visibility through additional displays or adjusted seating layout. 
 4. Participant Preparedness and Expectations 
 Observations: 
 Majority of students arrived on time and were well-oriented about the seminar’s objective. 
 Recommendations: 
 Encourage students to prepare questions beforehand to enrich discussions.
